About NovaSphere AI

NovaSphere AI is an AI-focused workspace designed to help people explore ideas, have conversations, organize projects, work with saved content and use creative tools in one place.

Our goal is to make advanced AI experiences easier to access and simpler to use across modern devices.

What NovaSphere offers

AI conversations

Ask questions, brainstorm and work through ideas.

Creative tools

Explore image and creative workflows.

Projects & library

Keep related ideas, chats and content organized.

Productivity

Use tools designed around everyday tasks and workflows.

Our approach

We aim to build useful experiences with clear controls, accessible design and continuously improving product features.
