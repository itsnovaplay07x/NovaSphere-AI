NovaSphere AI Features

Explore the main areas and workflows available in the NovaSphere AI experience.

A workspace for AI-powered work

NovaSphere AI brings multiple AI-oriented workflows into one product experience. The purpose is to make it easier to move from a question or idea to organized work without constantly switching between separate interfaces.

Conversations and ideas

Use the conversation workspace to ask questions, explore topics, brainstorm and develop ideas step by step.

Creative workflows

NovaSphere is designed to support creative exploration, including workflows where ideas can be developed into richer content.

Projects and organization

Keep related work together so ongoing ideas and tasks are easier to revisit and continue.

Modern device access

The interface is designed with responsive use in mind so the experience can adapt to different screen sizes.

Features can evolve

NovaSphere AI is an evolving product. Available tools and workflows may change as new versions are developed and existing features are improved.
