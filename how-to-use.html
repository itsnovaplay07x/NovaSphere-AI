How to Use NovaSphere AI

A simple introduction to starting a conversation and getting useful results from NovaSphere AI.

1. Open the workspace

Start from the NovaSphere AI home screen. Use the main input area to enter a question, task or idea you want to explore.

2. Be clear about your goal

Specific prompts usually produce more focused results. Include useful context, constraints, examples or the format you want when they matter.

3. Continue the conversation

You do not always need to start over. Follow up with corrections, extra details or a new question that builds on the previous context.

4. Review important information

AI-generated output can be useful for learning and productivity, but it may contain mistakes or outdated information. Verify important facts with reliable sources.

For financial, medical, legal, safety or other high-stakes decisions, do not rely solely on AI output. Seek qualified or authoritative sources when appropriate.
